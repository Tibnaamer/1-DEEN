About Me

For the last two decades, crafting nasheeds has been more than just a creative outlet—it has been a lifelong passion. Releasing music under the name 1 Deen, I have intentionally stayed anonymous so that the focus remains entirely on the words, the emotion, and the spiritual message rather than the person behind the microphone. This website is the sole official platform hosting my complete catalogue. Every piece featured here is a 100% vocals-only production, stripped back to pure voice and harmony. Whether you have been listening for years or are discovering my work for the first time, welcome to the journey.
